## Evidence and depiction boundaries

- The animal is a Galápagos marine iguana, not a green iguana, Galápagos land iguana, monitor lizard, crocodilian or imaginary marine reptile.
- Every frame keeps a short blunt snout, granular charcoal scales, a low row of conical dorsal spines, strong clawed limbs and a long tail without a fish fin.
- Frames 02 and 04 show different aquatic actions. Frame 02 uses the laterally compressed tail for swimming while the limbs stay near the body. Frame 04 uses the claws to hold basalt while the blunt snout reaches short algal turf.
- Frame 03 represents a connected volcanic intertidal environment. It does not identify an island, tide height, algal species or measured cover.
- Frame 05 shows one digging moment and no eggs. It does not encode a clutch size, nest depth, incubation duration or outcome.
- Frame 06 represents visual census work. It does not reproduce a real survey protocol, site, animal, observer, abundance estimate or result.
- The set uses common charcoal-gray coloration. It does not generalize the seasonal red-green coloration of some southern breeding males to the whole species.
