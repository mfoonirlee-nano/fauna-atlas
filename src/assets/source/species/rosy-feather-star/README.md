## Accepted observations and limits

Frame 01 shows one plausible red-and-cream adult on a temperate rocky wall. Ten feathered arms and the small central body remain visible, but the compressed arm bases do not document every internal ossicle or prove the identity of a field specimen. The image supplies no date, site, depth, current speed or body measurement.

Frame 02 shows ten complete pinnate arms arranged around a central calyx, with several hooked cirri under the body. The edit removed one extra two-arm sector. It cannot distinguish *A. bifida* from *A. petasus* by arm count alone, provide a cirrus count, resolve pinnule segments or serve as a diagnostic voucher.

Frame 03 reconstructs a feeding fan in bottom flow. The raised arms, pinnules and sparse particles express the documented suspension-feeding posture. A still image cannot measure current direction, flow speed, particle size, capture rate, feeding rhythm or the path of an individual particle.

Frame 04 shows a visible water gap between one feather star and the seabed. The accepted edit removes a short extra arm and leaves ten complete pinnate arms. One instant cannot reveal stroke sequence, which arms supplied thrust, swimming speed, distance, duration or whether a wild animal would adopt the same pose.

Frame 05 enlarges several proximal arm and pinnule regions so external embryo groups can be read. The image does not establish brood size, embryo age, fertilization time, spawning month, egg dimensions or the duration of attachment. The translucent mucus and embryo forms are reconstructions rather than microscope data.

Frame 06 depicts one plausible non-contact survey. The tape carries no readable values, and the ROV does not touch the animals. A single reconstructed pass cannot establish species identity for every distant animal, density, occupied area, population size, long-term trend or habitat condition.

No frame documents a field-observed event or establishes conservation status. The accepted files contain no visible labels, arrows, numbers, logos, signatures, borders or watermarks.
