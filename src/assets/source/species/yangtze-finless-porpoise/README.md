## Evidence boundaries

- The morphology anchor is *Neophocaena asiaeorientalis*: a compact gray freshwater porpoise with a blunt beakless head, one blowhole, pectoral flippers, a horizontal two-lobed tail fluke and no dorsal fin. A narrow flush tubercled midline ridge must not become a small fin, keel, zipper or armored crest.
- Images 01 and 02 came from fresh final generations after earlier candidates made the tail read vertically. The accepted files do not inherit an edit or reference-image chain. Their high-oblique views make the left–right fluke lobes and rear central notch readable.
- Image 03 depicts a generalized connected river–lake landscape rather than a named site, mapped corridor or measured water level. One small animal supplies scale without turning the frame into an abundance claim.
- Image 04 records only a reconstructed instant of breathing. The faint mist does not establish a breathing interval, dive duration, body condition or health state.
- Image 05 is a representative adult–calf scene. Appearance alone cannot confirm the adult's sex, kinship, calf age, nursing status or social relationship. The accepted high-oblique frame naturally occludes the adult's far pectoral flipper despite the generation prompt requesting four visible flippers; this is an ordinary viewpoint occlusion, not a dorsal fin or extra-anatomy artifact.
- Image 06 illustrates passive acoustic fieldwork. A hydrophone deployment does not by itself prove presence, absence, individual identity or abundance. The accepted image is a targeted correction of the first generation: a tiny pseudo-lettering mark was removed while people, equipment and composition stayed unchanged.
- All animal frames exclude a projecting beak, dorsal fin, visible sonar graphics, vertical fish tail, open mouth, exposed teeth and human-like smile. The monitoring frame deliberately contains zero animals.
