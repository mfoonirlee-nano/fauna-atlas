## Evidence boundaries

- The adult pattern is `Danaus plexippus`: orange wings divided by black veins, black margins with ordered white spots, and no Viceroy-like transverse hindwing line.
- The female cover has heavier veins and no hindwing androconial patches. The male identification frame has one black patch on each hindwing and relatively finer veins.
- Milkweeds are larval host plants. Adults use many nectar plants, so the cover does not imply that adult monarchs feed only on milkweed.
- The striped larva has one anterior and one posterior pair of fleshy filaments. They are not horns, antennae or stinging spines.
- The pupa is a naked chrysalis attached by a cremaster to a silk pad, not a woven cocoon.
- The migration frame represents a loose directional movement, not one individual completing the entire multi-generation annual cycle.
- The overwintering frame shows closed-wing roosting behavior in generalized oyamel forest and deliberately omits a named colony or exact location.
- The images are generated reconstructions, not occurrence records, specimens or proof of identification.
