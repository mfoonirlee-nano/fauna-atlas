## Evidence boundaries

- *Tubuca arcuata* is the current species anchor. The historical combination *Uca arcuata* links it to the TODO wording, but the pictures do not stand for every species once grouped under *Uca* sensu lato.
- The dark reticulated carapace, tall eyestalks and tuberculate red-orange-to-pale major chela are useful reconstruction anchors, not a substitute for locality, specimens and diagnostic examination. Color varies with individual, condition, light and mud cover.
- Adult males have one greatly enlarged chela and one small feeding chela; females have two small feeding chelae. The images do not imply that every limb remains equally visible from one camera angle.
- Image 03 was prompted for five animals, but the accepted frame contains four clearly visible crabs. Its product copy therefore says “数只” and makes no abundance claim.
- Image 04 represents surface-deposit feeding with one still instant. Pellets indicate processed sediment; they do not show exactly which particles were ingested or prove a fixed diet at every site.
- Image 05 freezes a vertical wave used in social and courtship contexts. It does not prove that a particular female responded or that every wave leads to mating.
- Image 06 combines burrow counts, photography and environmental measurement as a plausible monitoring scene. Its first result incorrectly left the salinity-and-temperature probe in exposed mud; a targeted edit moved the same probe into the tidal creek and repaired the original patch without changing the people, quadrat or crabs. Burrow openings are not converted directly into exact crab abundance, and no animal is captured or tagged.
