## Evidence and depiction boundaries

- Frames 01 and 02 show a generic adult Atlantic bluefin form. Frame 02 has the clearest short pectoral fins, two dorsal fins and posterior finlets; neither image can replace a specimen diagnosis or supply a measured maximum.
- Frames 03 through 05 show the first dorsal fin retracted into its dorsal groove during cruising. They do not demonstrate that the species has only one dorsal fin.
- Frame 03 shows seven fish together. It does not establish school size, speed, a migration route, stock identity or passage across the ICCAT 45°W management boundary.
- Frame 04 shows proximity to a school of small fish without a strike or capture. The prey cannot be identified to species and does not represent the complete diet.
- Frame 05 shows a seasonal aggregation only. It contains no eggs or gametes and cannot prove active spawning, sex, stock of origin or an exact spawning site.
- Frame 06 illustrates electronic tracking. The generated tag has a plausible scale and dorsal position, but the tether and anchor are not resolved, so the image does not document installation technique or a real study animal.
- The tail-peduncle keels are not sufficiently clear to serve as a diagnostic caption detail. No frame supplies a conservation assessment, abundance estimate, quota or management outcome.
- No frame contains text, a logo or a watermark.
